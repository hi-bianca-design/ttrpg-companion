# Market research: first round vs rerun vs Gemini

Written 2026-11-03. Compares the first-round docs ([Reddit pain points](reddit-pain-points.md), [content licensing](../legal/content-licensing.md), [revenue model](../business/revenue-model.md)) with the independent [rerun](market-research-rerun.md) and a third, Gemini deep-research report (not stored in the repo; its figures are secondary and some are unsourced).

**Decision:** keep the name Session Zero for now. The name risks below stay open.

## Where the sources agree

- **SRD attribution:** the wording is identical across the licensing doc and the rerun. No other Wizards attribution is allowed; "5E compatible" is allowed.
- **D&D Beyond:** about 4.75 stars from 92,665 iOS ratings. Its 2026 roadmap (2026-02-19) has no mobile, homebrew or live-session item. Annual-billing prices ($26 and $55 a year) match the checkout prices ($2.99 and $5.99 a month).
- **Sigil:** servers closed 2026-10-31, announced October 2025, about 30 staff laid off in March 2025. Three sources agree, which settles the date.
- **Name:** the Mythic Grove Class 28 mark (serial 98352984), Session Zero Labs, and no app named Session Zero on the US App Store.
- **Conversion:** about 2%. The rerun's source is RevenueCat 2026: 2.1% freemium, 10.7% hard paywall (all-category medians, not TTRPG).
- **Selling non-SRD content:** no official route found; the fan-content policy was seen only through search summaries.

## Differences

| Topic | Difference | Better source |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion rate | "About 2%, secondary" against a named RevenueCat figure | Rerun |
| Mythic Grove mark status | One directory says registered (reg. 7640275), another says pending; goods unconfirmed | Unresolved; the USPTO record was not reached |
| 2024 content missing in the app | Rerun cites 2024-25 forum threads; first round read 2026 threads and found only 2024-sheet bugs | First round for the present; the rerun's threads may be stale |
| Gemini satisfaction percentages, "public domain", "most players use phones" | Unsourced or wrong (CC BY is a licence, not public domain) | Ignore |

## Found only in the later rounds

- **Rerun:** SessionKeeper (AI session recorder, $3.99 to $24.99 a month, 4.8 stars from 94 ratings) and a pending Class 9 SESSIONKEEPER filing in the same field; Saga20; competitor prices (Foundry $50 once, PrismScrollDM $2.99); Roll20 bought Demiplane in June 2024; market-size figures; the Apple rule sections (1.2, 5.1.1(v), 3.1.1, 3.1.2(a), 3.1.3(b)).
- **Gemini:** sessionzero.games (Kejid), a free open-source group-voting tool for choosing a system, the closest same-name product so far; Daggerheart's own Session Zero page (Daggerheart is our second game system); Bag of Mapping; a cited r/onednd thread titled "New DDB mobile app" (id 1vbup00) that the report never discusses.

## Not confirmed by the rerun or Gemini

- Every Reddit theme in the first-round report (Reddit was blocked for the rerun).
- Demand for in-person phone-first play. The rerun says it found none stated; the first round found paper beats apps at many tables.
- The Daggerheart licence terms, and the first round's free-tier cap hypotheses.
- Session Zero Labs' feature list, and the card-deck goods for the Mythic Grove mark.

## Most worth acting on

1. **Run the official USPTO search** (SESSION ZERO, classes 9, 41 and 28, live and dead) and get a trademark lawyer's view. Add sessionzero.games and the Daggerheart page to the conflict list.
2. **Check the "no mobile on D&D Beyond's roadmap" claim** against the r/onednd thread and current release notes before using it in positioning.
3. **Show the SRD attribution in an About / Licences screen** before any public launch.

Also still open: validate DM demand with real DMs before fixing prices.
